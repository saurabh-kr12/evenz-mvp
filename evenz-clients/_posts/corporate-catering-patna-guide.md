## First, What Kind of Office Catering Do You Need?

"Catering" is a broad term. The first step is to know what to ask for.

* **1. Drop-Off Box Lunches:**
    * **What it is:** Individual, pre-packaged meals (like a premium thali or a box with a sandwich, salad, and dessert).
    * **Best For:** Quick team meetings, training sessions, or any event where 10-30 people need to eat efficiently without a full buffet setup.

* **2. Drop-Off Buffet:**
    * **What it is:** The most popular `office catering` option. The caterer drops off the food in professional, hot chafing dishes and sets up a simple buffet line. They come back a few hours later to pick it all up.
    * **Best For:** Team lunches, monthly all-hands meetings, or casual office parties (50-150 people).

* **3. Full-Service Catering:**
    * **What it is:** The "full package." This includes the food, the buffet setup, *and* a professional service staff who serves guests, manages the buffet, and handles all the cleanup.
    * **Best For:** Major events like an annual party, a big product launch, or a formal client dinner.

## The 3-Point Checklist for Hiring Any Corporate Caterer

Once you know what you need, how do you pick the right vendor? Don't get distracted by flashy menus. For `business catering`, these are the three things that *really* matter:

1.  **Punctuality is Everything.** For a 1:00 PM lunch meeting, 1:15 PM is a disaster. A true professional caterer understands this. They will arrive early to set up and be ready to serve *before* your guests are.
2.  **Can I Get a Proper GST Invoice?** This sounds boring, but it's a critical filter. Any serious, professional caterer will be able to provide a proper GST-compliant bill for your company's accounts department without any hassle. If they can't, they're not a real professional service.
3.  **Do They Fit the "Vibe"?** The caterer you'd hire for a 500-person wedding (like MunnaJi, who is fantastic at scale) might not be the right choice for a 10-person executive board meeting. You need a vendor that matches the scale and formality of your specific event.
