## What *Actually* Makes a "Top Caterer"?

Before we just throw names at you, what should you even be looking for? After talking to dozens of vendors, here's our simple checklist:

1.  **Do They Show Up? (Reliability is #1):** A great caterer is invisible. They're on time, the staff is professional, and everything just *works* without you having to worry.
2.  **Food & Hygiene (The Obvious One):** The food has to be amazing. Simple. But just as important: is it clean? Ask if they have an FSSAI license. It's a huge sign of professionalism.
3.  **Do They Fit *Your* Event?** Can they handle what you need? A great `catering for 20 people` service is different from a 500-person wedding buffet. You need one that fits your scale.
4.  **No Shady Bills:** Do they give you a clear, itemized quote? Or is it one big number with a bunch of "service charges" you don't understand? Transparency is key.
