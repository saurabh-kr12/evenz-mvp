## Breaking Down the "Per Plate" Deal

You'll hear this a lot: caterers usually charge **"per plate"** or **"per person."** Makes sense, right? This price *typically* covers the main stuff:

* The actual food (starters, main dishes, desserts, drinks)
* Basic setup (like the buffet tables and those warmer things)
* Standard plates, spoons, glasses, etc.
* The folks serving the food (waiters, people managing the buffet line)

**Quick Tip:** Always, *always* ask *exactly* what's included. Things like fancy live cooking stations (chaat, pasta, etc.), really nice crockery, or special decorations often cost extra. Better to know upfront!

## Patna Catering Prices: What to Expect (Roughly, for 2025)

Okay, let's talk numbers. Keep in mind, prices swing wildly based on who you hire, how fancy the menu is, and the service level. But here's a general idea for Patna:

* **Casual & Standard Gigs (Think birthday bashes, smaller parties, regular office lunches):**
    * **Veg:** Maybe ₹250 - ₹500 per head
    * **Non-Veg:** Around ₹350 - ₹650 per head
    * *What you usually get:* A solid menu with common dishes, standard buffet setup. Job done!

* **A Step Up (Engagements, nicer corporate events, medium-sized weddings):**
    * **Veg:** ₹500 - ₹900ish
    * **Non-Veg:** ₹650 - ₹1100ish
    * *What you usually get:* More variety, maybe a live counter included, food looks nicer, staff might be more polished.

* **Going All Out (Big fat weddings, fancy corporate parties):**
    * **Veg:** ₹900 up to ₹1500 or even more
    * **Non-Veg:** ₹1100 up to ₹2000 or beyond
    * *What you usually get:* Gourmet food, maybe international stuff, lots of live stations, looks incredible, super professional service, top-quality ingredients, maybe even custom menus.

**Just Remember:** These are *ballpark figures*. Prices change with the seasons (wedding season, anyone?), the exact dishes you pick, and all the things we'll talk about next.

## What Makes Catering Prices Go Up (or Down) in Patna?

So why the big difference between caterers? It boils down to a few key things:

1.  **Menu Choices:** Want something exotic or dishes that need super-skilled chefs? That'll cost more than crowd-pleasing North Indian staples.
2.  **How Much Food?** More variety = more cost. Simple as that. More starters, mains, desserts, live counters add up.
3.  **Veg vs. Non-Veg:** Chicken and mutton just cost more than veggies, so non-veg options usually bump up the per-plate price.
4.  **Guest Count:** Sometimes, for *really* big crowds (like 500+), you might get a slightly better rate. But for smaller, intimate gatherings, the per-plate cost could be higher, or there might be a minimum total bill.
5.  **How It's Served:** Sit-down dinners with waiters serving each table? That needs more staff than a self-service buffet and often costs more.
6.  **Live Counters:** Always a hit, but chaat stations, pasta bars, fancy mocktails usually mean extra charges.
7.  **Party Duration:** If the event runs longer, the caterer needs to pay their staff for more hours.
8.  **Location, Location, Location:** Is your venue far out? Hard to get to? They might add a bit for transportation and logistics.
9.  **Reputation Matters:** The well-known, highly-rated caterers in Patna often charge more because they have a proven track record.
